# Volleyball-Elo-System

A website for finding pickup volleyball games, both indoor and beach, and keeping track of how good everyone is.

- **Find a game.** See who's playing at local gyms and beach courts, and when. Join a game, or get in line to play the winners.
- **Get a rating.** Players have a rating, like in chess. It goes up when you win and down when you lose, depending on how strong your teammates and opponents are.
- **Play with friends.** Make a profile, add friends, and bring them into games.

This project is in early planning.
